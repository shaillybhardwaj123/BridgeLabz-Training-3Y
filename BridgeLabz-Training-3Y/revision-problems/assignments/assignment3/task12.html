Why Do Quotes Inspire Us?

Quotations inspire people because they compress complex human experiences, profound insights, and universal truths into concise, memorable statements. They act as mirrors reflecting our own struggles and aspirations, reminding us that others have walked similar paths. By capturing wisdom in a handful of powerful words, quotes motivate us to push past obstacles and view our lives from a fresh, positive perspective.
