Reserved Characters and Symbols

Here are the five basic XML/HTML reserved characters:

  • Less Than symbol: < (written as &lt;)
  • Greater Than symbol: > (written as &gt;)
  • Ampersand symbol: & (written as &amp;)
  • Double Quotation mark: " (written as &quot;)
  • Single Quotation / Apostrophe mark: ' (written as &apos; or &#39;)

Here are common copyright and trademark symbols:

  • Copyright symbol: © (written as &copy;)
  • Trademark symbol: ™ (written as &trade;)
  • Registered Trademark symbol: ® (written as &reg;)
