ques  10 block level elements 
Here are 10 common block-level elements in HTML:

1. **`<div>`** - Generic container for flow content.
2. **`<p>`** - Paragraph.
3. **`<h1>` to `<h6>`** - Section headings.
4. **`<ul>`** - Unordered list.
5. **`<ol>`** - Ordered list.
6. **`<li>`** - List item.
7. **`<section>`** - Semantic section of a document.
8. **`<article>`** - Self-contained article/content composition.
9. **`<header>`** - Introductory content or set of navigational links.
10. **`<footer>`** - Footer for its nearest sectioning content.
